The Met Fifth Avenue
How the building works
  • Fifth Avenue is east.Walking in from the Great Hall you face west, toward Central Park.
  • North: Egypt below, Asia above.Turn right from the Great Hall for Egypt, ending at the Temple of Dendur.
  • South: Greece and Rome below, Near East and Islamic world above.Greek and Roman Art leads on to Africa, Oceania and the Americas.
  • Center: Medieval below, European paintings above.The Grand Staircase lands you at the Old Masters. The American Wing fills the northwest corner on both floors.
